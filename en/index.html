Why WaveScope

Teams working with many Instagram creators keep running into the same three problems.

01

Manual reporting

Final reports are built by screenshotting posts and copying numbers one by one. It is slow and error-prone.

02

Unfair comparisons

Posts go live at different times, some are boosted with ads, and "views" mean different things. Putting them side by side leads to misleading conclusions.

03

Choosing without data

When picking the next creators or the content to boost, there is no objective, comparable data at hand.

Features

From registering collaboration posts and capturing data to comparing creators and monitoring topics, all in one place.

Campaign and collaboration management

Manage campaigns, creators, collaborations and agreed deliverables. Paste an Instagram URL to register a post; delivery progress is calculated automatically.

Scheduled capture and daily changes

Snapshots of each post are saved on a schedule, so daily deltas, 7-day moving averages and anomalies are always available.

Fair comparison at evaluation points

Every collaboration is compared on day 1, 3, 7, 14 and so on after publishing, with cost per engagement (CPE) as the primary metric. Numbers measured differently never share a comparison table.

Relative performance

Compare a collaboration post against the same creator's regular posts to see whether sponsored content underperforms.

Creator observation and selection support

Observe creator accounts before any collaboration. Enter a quote to get an estimated CPE, and get a shortlist of content worth boosting.

Topic volume monitoring

Define topics by Instagram hashtags, track volume and anomalies, and see how topic volume moves during a campaign.

Our data principles

Numbers you can trust matter more than numbers that look good.

  • 1

    Official Meta APIs only, no scraping

    Data comes only from Instagram's official APIs and from data creators choose to authorize. Anything the official APIs do not provide is shown as "not available" and never filled in by other means.

  • 2

    Sources stored separately, never merged

    Public data, creator-authorized data and manual input are stored and labeled separately. Paid (boosted) views are never mixed with organic views.

  • 3

    No composite scores

    Weights hide subjective assumptions and bury signals that need human judgment. We provide the full metrics and multi-dimensional views, and leave the judgment to you.
